How We Work

4 Simple Steps to Your Dream Interior

Our streamlined 4-step workflow ensures zero surprises, strict deadline tracking, and pristine craftsmanship.

01

Site Measurement

Our lead designer visits your site in Raipur to measure dimensions and study light & ventilation flow.

02

3D Visualization

We generate photorealistic 3D renders of your living room, kitchen & false ceiling before work begins.

03

Material Curation

Select wallpapers, PVC marble sheets, acrylic laminates, and hardware at our Raipura Chowk studio.

04

Turnkey Handover

Our master technicians complete fitting, cove light wiring, and cleaning for a seamless move-in.
