Answers & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our interior design workflow, material durability, or timeline in Raipur? We have answers.

What areas in Chhattisgarh do you cover for site visits & turnkey execution?

Our main interior design studio is situated at Raipura Chowk, Mahadev Ghat Road, Raipur. We regularly service residential and commercial projects across Raipur (VIP Road, Shankar Nagar, Devendra Nagar, Sadar Bazaar, Tatibandh), Bhilai, Durg, and surrounding regions in Chhattisgarh.

Is the initial site visit and design consultation free?

Yes! We provide complimentary initial site measurements and design consultations across Raipur. Our lead spatial designer visits your site to understand requirements before presenting a tailored scope of work.

What is the advantage of PVC Marble sheets & Fluted Louvers over real marble?

PVC Marble sheets and timber fluted louvers offer 100% termite proof, moisture-resistant, lightweight, and economical alternatives to heavy Italian marble and wood. They are installed in 1-2 days without messy wet civil work, creating instant high-gloss feature walls.

Do you provide 3D visualization renders before starting physical work?

Absolutely. We craft photorealistic 3D spatial renders of your living room, modular kitchen, master bedroom, and false ceiling lighting layout so you can preview colors, finishes, and spatial fitment prior to execution.

How long does a full home turnkey interior project typically take?

Depending on scope (e.g., modular kitchen + PVC wall paneling vs full 3BHK turnkey fitment), most projects in Raipur are completed between 18 to 35 working days. We adhere strictly to agreed handover schedules.
